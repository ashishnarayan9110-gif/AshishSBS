Who’s behind it

Based in Chandigarh, working across Himachal. Furniture first, everything else after.

I started with furniture. A bench, hand tools, and the unforgiving fact that a joint either closes or it doesn’t. You cannot argue a drawer into fitting. That taught me more about building software than any framework has.

Then classrooms — explaining something properly is the fastest way to discover you only half understood it. Then companies, where the same rule applies at a larger scale, with other people’s money attached and far less patience in the room.

What carries across all four is a single habit: build the smallest real version, put it in front of reality, and write down what happened. Including when what happened was that it failed. A portfolio of only successes is a sales page. A record of both is evidence.

01 — The premise

A record, not a portfolio. A portfolio shows the things that worked. This shows all of it, which is the only version worth anything to someone deciding whether to trust me with their money, their timber, or a room full of their students.

Fourteen entries across four benches, built over seven years. Wood, code, classrooms, companies. The same question each time, answered at a different speed: did it hold, or did it gap?

04 — The two that gapped

Betta Beaut, 2023. Salon technology infrastructure. The right problem and the wrong buyer — the people feeling the pain weren’t the ones holding the budget. Eighteen months, no second round.

Folding Wall Table, 2022. A wall-mounted drop-leaf for a small flat. The hinge geometry was wrong under load and it sagged within a month. Rebuilt twice, then abandoned.
